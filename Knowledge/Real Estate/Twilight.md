# Twilight

Positioned as the premium emotional-appeal add-on, not a standard inclusion:
- **Twilight Premium** package = twilight photography + video + aerial coverage — "premium dusk
  look and stronger emotional appeal" (`Brand/Packages.md`).
- **Signature Package** bundles twilight photography alongside everything else as the top-tier
  offer.
- Golden hour is grouped with twilight in `services.html`'s photography feature list.

Not yet documented: exact shoot-window timing rules (minutes before/after sunset), interior
light-on staging checklist for twilight interiors, or weather/cloud contingency policy. Worth
adding once defined — this is exactly the kind of fact a "Book Shoot" automation button
(`Software/Roadmap.md` Phase 4) would need to check sunset time and flag the shoot window
automatically.

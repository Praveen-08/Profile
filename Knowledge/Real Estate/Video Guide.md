# Video Guide

From `services.html`: "Story-driven property films that captivate buyers from the first frame...
smooth camera movement, natural light, and licensed music."
- 4K cinematic walkthroughs.
- 48-hour delivery.
- Social media cuts included with the Video & Photo Package.

## How QuickReel codifies this
`quickreel/` (see `Brand/Editing Style.md` for the engine breakdown) is effectively "Video Guide
as software": its `story-engine`, `camera-engine`, and `hook-engine` packages encode the same
story-driven, first-frame-hooks-the-buyer philosophy stated above, but automated from a set of
already-edited photos rather than live-shot 4K footage. As QuickReel matures, treat it as the
place these video-editing rules get formalized and versioned, not just this markdown file.

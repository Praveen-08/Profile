# Agent Reels

- **Signature Reel Add-On** package: "Premium short-form vertical reel for Instagram, Facebook,
  TikTok and agent branding" — explicitly framed as helping the *agent's personal brand*, not
  just the listing (`Brand/Packages.md`).
- Delivered as part of the Signature Package's bundle too.

## QuickReel output targets
`quickreel/README.md` confirms the technical target: 1080x1920 (vertical) beat-synced video,
exported per-platform. Music selection comes from a 10-vibe catalog (`music-engine`), matched to
beats via `beat-engine`/`timing-engine`.

## Content ideas cadence (from the master roadmap's "Marketing GPT" daily brief)
Suggested daily output shape for agent-facing reel/caption content: 5 hooks, 5 reels, 2 stories,
1 carousel, 1 blog post, 1 email. See `Business/Instagram.md` and `Business/Facebook.md` for
platform-specific notes.

# Photography Guide

Baseline standard for every PK Visuals shoot (`services.html`):
- HDR interior & exterior shots as default, not an upsell.
- Professional lighting setup (supplemented, not just available light).
- Twilight & golden hour available as a premium add-on (`Twilight.md`).
- 24-hour delivery is a marketed commitment, not just an internal target.
- Unlimited web-ready exports included — no per-photo licensing friction for the agent.

Package-tier photo counts (`Packages.md`):
- Essential Media: up to 25 photos, HDR editing included.
- Video & Photo Package: up to 35 photos HDR.
- Signature Package: full coverage, no stated cap (bundled premium).

See `Composition.md` and `Lighting.md` for the shot-by-shot craft rules, and `Brand/Editing
Style.md` for the post-production side.

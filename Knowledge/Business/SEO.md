# SEO

- `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` at repo root.
- Page `<title>`s and meta descriptions on `index.html`, `services.html`, `work.html`,
  `book.html` — all Auckland-specific, keyword-rich for "real estate photography Auckland".
- schema.org `LocalBusiness` structured data on `index.html` with phone, email, address locality
  (Auckland), and price range.

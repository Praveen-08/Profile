## Services (from `services.html`)
1. **Real Estate Photography** — HDR interior/exterior, professional lighting, twilight/golden
   hour, 24hr delivery, unlimited web-ready exports.
2. **Cinematic Videography** — 4K story-driven walkthroughs, smooth camera movement, licensed
   music, 48hr delivery.
3. **Aerial & Drone** — CAA-licensed operator, 4K aerial photo/video, neighbourhood context,
   land boundary coverage, 24hr delivery.
4. **Floor Plans** — 2D & 3D, accurate dimensions, furnished/unfurnished versions, 24hr delivery.
5. **Virtual Staging** — digitally furnished vacant rooms, multiple style options, 48hr
   turnaround.

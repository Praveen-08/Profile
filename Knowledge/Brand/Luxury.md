# Luxury Positioning

Signals already in the codebase that define "luxury" for this brand — keep new work consistent
with these, don't reinvent luxury cues per-project:

- **Colour**: near-black background, warm off-white text, single muted-gold accent
  (`Brand/Colours.md`) — no bright/saturated secondary colours.
- **Type**: serif (`Cormorant Garamond`) for anything that needs to feel premium/editorial;
  sans (`Inter`) kept strictly to functional UI. Mixing the two is the luxury signal — don't run
  an entire page in sans.
- **Pacing/copy**: short, confident sentences; outcome language ("win listings," "sell faster")
  over feature dumps.
- **Top-tier package framing**: the "Signature Package" (`Brand/Packages.md`) is explicitly
  badged "Most Complete" and priced as a bundle rather than an itemised add-on stack — luxury
  packages read as curated, not assembled.
- **Delivery as a luxury signal, not just an ops metric**: 24hr/48hr delivery times are marketed
  prominently — speed-with-polish is part of the premium promise, not a discount-brand trait.

Apply this same restraint to any AI-generated output (reels, captions, video style presets):
default to understated and precise over loud and effects-heavy.

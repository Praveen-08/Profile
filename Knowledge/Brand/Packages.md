# Media Packages

Source: `index.html` and `services.html` (`#packages` sections). All packages include 24hr photo
delivery and files ready for TradeMe, realestate.co.nz, and social media.

## Essential Media
Photography + floor plan + aerial images.
- Up to 25 photos, HDR editing included
- Floor plan, 24hr photo delivery, web & print ready
- Best for: standard residential listings needing polished, listing-ready media.

## Video & Photo Package
Photography + cinematic video + drone coverage.
- Up to 35 photos HDR, cinematic walkthrough video, drone coverage
- 48hr video delivery, social media cuts included
- Best for: listings needing stronger buyer attention across portals and social.

## Twilight Premium
Twilight photography + video + aerial coverage.
- Best for: listings needing a premium dusk look and stronger emotional appeal.

## Signature Reel Add-On
Premium short-form vertical reel for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and agent branding.
- Best for: agents building personal brand / stronger property attention.

## Signature Package ("Most Complete")
Premium photography + floor plan + aerial images + cinematic video + twilight photography +
signature social media reel.
- Best for: premium homes, competitive campaigns, agents wanting maximum listing impact without
  stacking individual add-ons.

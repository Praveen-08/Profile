# Logo Rules

Assets in the repo (root and `pkvisuals/assets/`):
- `logo-horizontal.png` — wide lockup, for nav bars / letterheads
- `logo-stacked.jpg` — stacked lockup, for square/social placements
- `logo-icon.jpg` — icon-only mark, for favicons / small UI
- `faviconp.ico` — production favicon

Wordmark from `footer.html` markup: **"PK" + "Visuals"** rendered as two weights/colours in the
same lockup (`footer__brand-name` = `PK<span> Visuals</span>`) — "PK" carries the primary weight,
"Visuals" is the lighter secondary word. Keep that two-part treatment in any new lockup rather
than treating "PK Visuals" as one flat string.
